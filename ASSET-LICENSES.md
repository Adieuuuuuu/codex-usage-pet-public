## Public notification sound

`src/renderer/sounds/hook-notification.mp3` was synthesized specifically for
the public snapshot from two short sine tones. It contains no sampled audio and
is dedicated to the public domain under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

The private working build used a different reference sound. That third-party
sound is intentionally not included here.
